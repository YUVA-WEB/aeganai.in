🕰️ Our History

AEGAN ANEGAN INDUSTRIES PRIVATE LIMITED carries a proud legacy that began over three decades ago.

Born as LEW
🚀1991 – The Beginning
Our journey started as Loganathan Engineering Works, founded by Mr. Loganathan, a passionate and skilled engineer. The firm specialized in:
• 🚜 Tractor Trailers
• 🌾 Agricultural Implements like Rotavators and Cultivators
• 🛠️ Repair and Maintenance Services for Agro Machines
This foundation was built on hard work, craftsmanship, and service to the farming community.
Search for next Step
🛢️ 2015 – Vishnu Engineering – Entering the Oil Extraction Machinery Sector
With growing experience and customer trust, we ventured into manufacturing Wooden and Steel Rotary Oil Extraction Machines, also known as Marachekku Machines. These machines promote healthy, chemical-free oil production and gained popularity for their quality and durability.
Next Big Step
🏗️ 2018 Onwards – Innovation & Industrial Expansion
Led by Yuvaraja, the second generation, the business embraced innovation and shifted focus towards:
• 🌿 Organic and cold-pressed oil machinery
• 🏗️ Industrial and construction equipment R&D
• 🧱 Heavy-duty machine fabrication & hydraulic systems
• 🤝 Supply & service for earth-moving machinery
AAI era Begins
🏢 2022 – Birth of Aegan Anegan Industries Pvt. Ltd.
To meet modern industrial demands and expand globally, we officially formed Aegan Anegan Industries Private Limited. Built with the vision to "Blend Tradition with Technology", we now serve:
• Agro Startups
• MSMEs
• Exporters of Natural Products
• Organic Oil Brands
• Industrial Machine Buyers
AAI Achivement
🏢 2025 – we are proud to have
• Served 33+ satisfied customers across Tamil Nadu and beyond
• Successfully installed both wooden and steel rotary oil extraction machines
• Entered into R&D for construction, industrial, and agri-machinery
• Assisted clients in availing government subsidies like PMEGP, NEEDS, PMFME
• Received trust and repeated orders from entrepreneurs, farmers, and MSMEs

✅ Legacy Credit: We proudly honor our origin — Loganathan Engineering Works — and continue its spirit in everything we do.

"Innovating Today, Creating Tomorrow for a Healthier Future."
