About Us

• Aegan Anegan Industries Private Limited is built on a legacy of trust, innovation, and engineering excellence. Our roots trace back to 1991, when Mr. Loganathan founded Loganathan Engineering Works, specializing in tractor trailers, rotavators, cultivators, and various agricultural equipment manufacturing and service.
• In 2015, we began manufacturing oil extraction machines, focusing on quality, durability, and health-conscious design. With evolving expertise and the visionary leadership of Mr. Yuvaraja, we transformed and expanded our operations into a full-fledged private limited company — Aegan Anegan Industries Private Limited.

🌱 Our Purpose

We aim to support agricultural entrepreneurs, organic product brands, and oil producers by delivering machines that ensure purity, performance, and sustainability.

🔰 Legacy + Innovation

From a humble workshop in 1991 to a modern industry leader, we carry forward the passion and precision of our founder with a bold commitment to "Make in India" innovation.
